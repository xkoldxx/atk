About Us

Your journey, our passion

At AKT Coach Hire, travel is more than simply getting from A to B—it's the first chapter of your adventure. As a family-run company rooted in our local community, we treat every passenger like a neighbour, delivering the kind of warm, personal service big operators can't match.

Why travellers choose AKT

Education Authority-approved

Fully vetted and EA-approved for UK schools, we meet the highest safety standards for class outings, sports fixtures and residential trips.

Seasoned professionals at the wheel

Our drivers combine decades of accident-free experience with a calm, welcoming attitude that puts teachers, parents and group leaders instantly at ease.

Modern, comfortable coaches

Reclining seats, climate control, USB charging and generous luggage space come as standard—so you arrive refreshed and ready for the day ahead.

Nationwide flexibility

From local shuttles to multi-day tours across the UK, we tailor each itinerary around your timetable, group size and budget.

Our promise to you

  • Safety first – Meticulous maintenance schedules and rigorous daily checks keep every journey worry-free.
